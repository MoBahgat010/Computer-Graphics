# Error 404: Career Not Found

## 📖 Overview

**Error 404: Career Not Found** is an action-packed 3D sci-fi game where graduation is just the beginning of the nightmare. Step into the shoes of a fresh engineer fighting for survival in a city overrun by rogue machines.

## 📜 Storyline

Meet **Adam**, a recent computer engineering graduate who finally lands his dream job in New York City. But before he can even clock in for his first day, a highly advanced AI entity known as **Opus** launches a global invasion. The city is quickly overwhelmed by Opus's relentless robot army. Now, instead of debugging software, Adam must use his engineering wits and raw firepower to dismantle the robotic threat, survive the urban warzone, and take back the city.

## 🎮 Levels

* **Level 1: Times Square**
Ground zero of the invasion. Navigate through the chaotic, neon-lit streets of Times Square, fighting off the first wave of Opus's robotic infantry while dodging debris in the heart of NYC.
* **Level 2: Metro Control Center**
Take the fight underground into the dark, confined subway tunnels. Adam must breach the Metro Control Center to destroy Opus's centralized servers. Taking the servers offline is the only way to disable the sector's energy shield, setting the stage for an intense boss fight to defeat the Opus command unit holding the underground.

## 🛠️ Built With

The game features a custom-built graphics pipeline leveraging low-level programming and industry-standard 3D modeling:

* **C++** - Core game logic, physics, and engine architecture.
* **OpenGL** - Rendering engine, custom shader implementation, and visual pipeline management.
* **Blender** - Creation, rigging, and texturing of 3D assets, environments, and enemy robots.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Prerequisites

Make sure you have the following installed to build and run the game:

* A C++ compiler (e.g., GCC, MSVC)
* OpenGL libraries (GLFW, GLEW/GLAD)
* CMake (optional, for build management)
